## 1. About the Website

The UCOSA-NA website is the official online platform for the **Ugbeka College Old Students' Association — North America**. It provides:

- Information about the association, its constitution, and leadership.
- A gallery of events and member photos.
- A secure **Members Portal** where you can view your dues, endowment contributions, and association meeting notes.

**Website address:** `https://ucosa-na.org`

## 3. Creating Your Account

Member accounts are created by an administrator — you cannot self-register. To become a member:

1. Contact the association at `admin@ucosa-na.org` or reach out to any executive member.
2. Once your membership is approved, an administrator will create your account.
3. You will receive a **Welcome Email** at your registered email address containing:
   - Your login email address
   - A temporary password
   - Instructions for your first login

Keep this email safe. The temporary password expires if not used promptly.

---

## 4. Logging In

1. Go to `https://ucosa-na.org`.
2. Click **Member Login** in the navigation bar (or scroll to the login section on the home page).
3. Enter your **email address** and **password**.
4. Click **Sign In**.

On successful login you will be taken to the **Members Portal** (`/members.html`).

### Login Failures

- If you enter the wrong password, you will see an error message showing how many attempts you have remaining.
- After **5 failed attempts**, your account is temporarily locked for **10 minutes**. Wait and try again.
- Every failed login attempt triggers a security alert to your email (and SMS if you have a phone number on file). See [Section 11](#11-security-alerts).

### Account Issues

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Your account has been suspended." | Your access has been suspended by an administrator. | Contact `admin@ucosa-na.org`. |
| "Your account has been locked." | A security lock was placed on your account. | Contact `admin@ucosa-na.org`. |
| "Your temporary password has expired." | You did not use your first-login temp password in time. | Contact an administrator for a new password reset. |

## 5. First Login — Setting Your Password

The first time you log in with your temporary password, you will be redirected to the **Change Password** page automatically. You must set a new personal password before accessing the portal.

**Password requirements:**
- At least **8 characters** long
- At least **one uppercase letter** (A–Z)
- At least **one special character** (e.g. `! @ # $ % ^ & *`)

**Steps:**
1. Enter your new password in the **New Password** field.
2. Re-enter it in the **Confirm Password** field.
3. Click **Set Password**.
4. You will be redirected to the Members Portal automatically.

You will also receive a confirmation email and SMS (if applicable) that your password was changed.

## 7. Viewing Your Financial Records

### 7.1 Annual Dues

The **Annual Dues** section shows your dues payment history by year, including:

- **Year** — the dues year
- **Amount** — the dues amount for that year (standard: $100.00)
- **Status** — Paid, Partial, or Unpaid
- **Due Date** — June 1th of each year

**How to pay:** Make your payment via **Zelle** to:
> **ucosa.northamerica@gmail.com**

After payment, contact the treasurer or a financial secretary to have your record updated.

### 7.2 Endowment Fund

The **Endowment Fund** section shows your contributions to the association's endowment fund, including:

- Contribution amount
- Contribution date
- Year
- Status (Paid / Partial / Unpaid)
- Payment method
- Notes

**How to contribute:** Same as dues — send payment via Zelle to `ucosa.northamerica@gmail.com` and notify the treasurer.

### 7.3 Payment Reminders

If your dues are unpaid as the due date approaches, the system will automatically send you:

- An **email and SMS reminder 30 days before June 1th** (around May 2th).
- An **email and SMS reminder on June 1th** (due date).

If you have already paid, please ignore the automated reminders. Your treasurer will update your record.

## 8. Meeting Notes

The **Meeting Notes** section lists official association meeting documents uploaded by the secretary.

Each entry shows:
- Meeting title
- Meeting date
- Upload date

**To view a document:**
- Click **View** to open the PDF in a new browser tab.

**To download a document:**
- Click **Download** to save the PDF to your device.

Meeting notes are available to all logged-in members.

## 9. Updating Your Contact Information

You can update your **phone number** and **mailing address** directly from the Members Portal.

1. In the portal, locate the **Profile / Contact Info** section.
2. Edit your **Phone** and/or **Address** fields.
3. Click **Save**.

Your name, email address, year joined, and graduation year can only be updated by an administrator. Contact `admin@ucosa-na.org` for those changes.

---

## 10. Changing Your Password

You can change your password at any time.

1. Click **Change Password** in the top navigation bar of the Members Portal.
2. You will be taken to `/change-password.html`.
3. Enter your **current password**.
4. Enter and confirm your **new password** (must meet the requirements listed in Section 5).
5. Click **Change Password**.

You will receive a confirmation **email and SMS** that your password was changed. If you did not initiate this change, contact `admin@ucosa-na.org` immediately.

---

## 11. Security Alerts

The system sends automatic security alerts to protect your account:

### Failed Login Alert
If someone attempts to log in to your account with the wrong password, you receive:
- An **email** with the IP address, approximate location, and time of the attempt.
- An **SMS** (if your phone is on file) with the same information.

If the attempt was yours (e.g. you mistyped your password), no action is needed. If you did not attempt to log in, **change your password immediately** at `https://ucosa-na.org/change-password.html` and contact `admin@ucosa-na.org`.

### Password Change Confirmation
Every time your password is changed, you receive a confirmation email and SMS. If you did not make this change, contact the administrator immediately.

## 12. Troubleshooting

### I did not receive my welcome email.
- Check your spam/junk folder.
- Ensure the email address registered with UCOSA-NA is correct.
- Contact `admin@ucosa-na.org` to have the welcome email resent or your password reset.

### I forgot my password.
- Contact an administrator at `admin@ucosa-na.org` to request a password reset.
- A new temporary password will be emailed to you.

### My account is locked or suspended.
- Contact `admin@ucosa-na.org` and provide your name and registered email address.

### My financial records look incorrect.
- Dues and endowment records are managed by the Financial Secretary.
- Contact the treasurer or `admin@ucosa-na.org` with your payment details (date, amount, method) for correction.

### I cannot view a meeting note PDF.
- Ensure your browser allows pop-ups from `ucosa-na.org`.
- Try using a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
- Try downloading the file instead of viewing it in-browser.

### The website is showing a maintenance notice.
- The site is temporarily offline for maintenance. Try again in a few minutes.
- For urgent matters, contact `admin@ucosa-na.org`.
