1 — Automated / Scheduled Notifications
# Notification Name Trigger / Schedule Recipients Channel Content Summary
1 Annual Dues Population Jan 1 — 12:01 AM ET (Automatic) System only (no notification sent) System Creates annual dues records for all active members for the new year. No email is sent — internal DB operation only.
2 Annual Dues 30-Day Advance Reminder May 2 — 9:00 AM ET (Automatic) All active members with unpaid dues for the current year Email SMS Reminds members their annual dues ($100 USD) are due in 30 days on June 1. Includes due date, amount, status, and Zelle payment details (ucosa.northamerica@gmail.com).
3 Annual Dues Due Date Reminder Jun 1 — 9:00 AM ET (Automatic) All active members with unpaid dues for the current year Email SMS Final reminder that dues are due today. Same details as advance reminder. Members who already paid are excluded automatically.
4 Birthday Greeting 1st of every month — 9:00 AM ET (Automatic) All active members whose birthday month is the current month Email SMS "Happy Birthday! Welcome to your birthday month of [Month]!" — includes a birthday message and a short prayer from the executive. Member's photo appears in the Highlights pane on the welcome page for the whole month.
5 90-Day Inactivity Reminder Daily — 8:00 AM ET (Automatic) Active members who have not logged in for 90+ days (and have not received this reminder in the past 90 days) Email SMS "We observed you have not logged in to your UCOSA account in 90 days." Includes last login date, list of what they are missing, and a login button. Reminder is not repeated for another 90 days after sending.
2 — Member Account Notifications
# Notification Name Trigger Recipient Channel Content Summary
6 Welcome / New Member Created Admin creates a new member account (single or bulk import) New member Email SMS "Welcome to UCOSA-North America!" Includes login URL (ucosa-na.org), email address, and temporary password. Password expires in 48 hours. Member must change password on first login.
7 Activation Reminder (Never Logged In) Admin clicks "Send Activation Reminders" in the Unactivated Members panel All active members who have never logged in (last_login is null) Email SMS "Action Required: Your account has not been activated." A new temporary password is generated and reset. Includes login URL, email, and new temporary password expiring in 48 hours.
8 Password Reset by Admin Admin clicks "Reset Password" on a member's record The affected member Email SMS "Your UCOSA-NA account password has been reset by an administrator." Includes new temporary password and login URL. Password expires in 48 hours.
9 Password Changed Confirmation Member successfully changes their own password The member who changed their password Email "Your UCOSA-NA password was successfully changed." Security confirmation with timestamp. Advises member to contact admin if they did not make this change.
10 Forgot Password — Reset Link Member clicks "Forgot Password" on the login page The requesting member Email Sends a secure one-time password reset link valid for a limited time. Member clicks the link to set a new password.
11 Failed Login Alert Multiple failed login attempts detected The account owner / admin Email "Failed Login Attempt — UCOSA-NA." Security alert notifying the member of an unsuccessful login attempt on their account.
12 Account Suspended Admin deactivates or locks a member account The affected member Email "Your UCOSA-NA account has been suspended." Informs the member their account has been deactivated and advises them to contact the executive for resolution.
3 — Financial Notifications
# Notification Name Trigger Recipient Channel Content Summary
13 Annual Dues Payment Receipt Admin records an annual dues payment for a member The paying member Email SMS Itemized payment receipt showing year, amount paid, payment method, date, and transaction reference. Confirms payment was received and recorded.
14 Annual Dues Individual Reminder Admin clicks "Send Reminder" on a specific member's dues record The selected member Email SMS Personalized dues reminder for the member showing their current dues balance, due date, status, and Zelle payment instructions.
15 Bulk Annual Dues Reminder Admin clicks "Send All Reminders" in the Annual Dues panel All active members with unpaid/partial dues for the selected year Email SMS Same as individual reminder but sent in bulk to all outstanding members at once.
16 Endowment Fund Payment Receipt Admin records an endowment fund contribution for a member The contributing member Email SMS Receipt for endowment fund contribution. Includes amount, year, payment method, and reference. Thanks the member for their contribution.
17 Endowment Fund Reminder Admin sends a reminder for outstanding endowment fund contributions The selected member Email SMS "UCOSA-NA Endowment Fund Reminder." Notifies a member of their outstanding endowment fund balance and provides payment details.
18 Special Levy / Member Donation Receipt Admin records a special levy or member donation payment The paying member Email SMS Payment receipt for Special Levy, Voluntary Contribution, or Member-Donation. Includes levy type, year, amount, and donation code.
19 Online Donation Receipt (Stripe) Donor completes an online donation via Stripe on the Donate page The donor (member or public) Email "Thank You for Your Donation — UCOSA-NA." Confirms the online donation, includes amount, Stripe payment reference, and a thank-you message from the association.

# Notification Name Trigger Recipients Channel Content Summary
20 SMS Broadcast Admin / PRO composes and sends from the SMS Broadcast panel All active members with a phone number on file SMS Free-form SMS message drafted by the admin or PRO officer. Used for urgent announcements, meeting reminders, or general association communications.
21 Email Broadcast Admin / PRO composes and sends from the Email Broadcast panel All active members Email Free-form email with custom subject and body drafted by admin or PRO. Used for newsletters, event announcements, executive communications, or general updates.
22 Meeting Notes Upload Notification Admin uploads a new meeting minutes document All active members Email SMS "New meeting notes have been uploaded." Notifies all members that minutes for a specific meeting date are available. Members must log in to view the document.
23 Invitational Broadcast Email Admin sends an invitation email to a list of non-member email addresses Prospective members / invitees (external recipients) Email "We Miss You — Come Back Home to UCOSA-NA." Invitation email sent to alumni or former contacts to encourage them to join or rejoin the association.

6 — Delivery Channel & Configuration Reference
Channel Provider Configuration Notes
Email SendGrid SENDGRID_API_KEY environment variable. Sender: ucosa.northamerica@gmail.com Failed emails are logged in the Email Failures panel in the admin portal. Delivery to international addresses (e.g. Nigeria) is subject to ISP filtering.
SMS Twilio (primary) / Vonage (fallback) TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID, TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN, TWILIO_PHONE_NUMBER. Vonage: VONAGE_API_KEY, VONAGE_API_SECRET. SMS is sent only when a phone number is on the member's profile. The system falls back to Vonage if Twilio fails. Nigerian (+234) numbers currently require carrier-level A2P registration. 7 — Procedures: How to Trigger Each Notification Type
Task Who Can Do It Steps
Add a new member and send welcome credentials Admin, Security Role Admin Portal → Create Member → fill in name, email, phone, role → Save. System automatically sends welcome email + SMS with temporary password.
Send activation reminders to members who never logged in Admin, Security Role Admin Portal → Unactivated Members → Refresh → Send Activation Reminders. System resets passwords and sends email + SMS to all listed members.
Reset a specific member's password Admin, Security Role Admin Portal → Member List → find member → Reset Password. System generates a new temporary password and emails + SMS the member.
Record a dues payment and send receipt Admin, Financial Role Admin Portal → Annual Dues → find member → Record Payment → enter amount, method, date → Save. Receipt is emailed + SMS'd automatically.
Send a dues reminder to one member Admin, Financial Role Admin Portal → Annual Dues → find member → Send Reminder. Email + SMS sent immediately.
Send dues reminders to all unpaid members Admin, Financial Role Admin Portal → Annual Dues → Send All Reminders button at the top of the section.
Send a broadcast message to all members Admin, PRO Role Admin Portal → PRO → SMS Broadcast (for SMS) or Email Broadcast (for email) → compose message → Send.
Notify members of new meeting notes Admin, Security Role Admin Portal → Meeting Notes → Upload Document → select file and set date → Upload. System automatically notifies all members by email + SMS.
Set a member's birthday month Admin only Admin Portal → Member Birthdays → find member → select month from dropdown → enter photo path (e.g. /ucosa_na_members/06-Ihaza.jpg) → Save. System will send birthday greeting on the 1st of that month and display their photo in the Highlights pane all month.
Send invitational emails to prospective members Admin, PRO Role Admin Portal → PRO → Broadcast Email (Invitational) → paste list of email addresses → compose message → Send.
