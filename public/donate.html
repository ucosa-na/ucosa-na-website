Back home at Ugbeka College, hundreds of bright, determined students wake up every day with one goal — to learn, grow, and build a better future.

Thanks to generous support, we have already taken a major step forward by building a computer laboratory. But building it was just the beginning.

To truly make a lasting impact, the lab must be maintained, upgraded, and actively used to equip students with real digital skills.

Right now, we need support to:

  • Create an environment where students can truly thrive

These students are ready to learn. They are eager, focused, and full of potential — but they need consistent access and proper guidance to thrive in today's digital world.

Your donation will not just support a facility — it will empower minds, build skills, and open doors to opportunities that can change lives forever.

No amount is too small. Every contribution helps keep the lab running and ensures these students are not left behind.
